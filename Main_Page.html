My Teachers

The methods that shape my work come from pioneers who've dared to see healing differently.

Dick Schwartz

Internal Family Systems (IFS)

A revolutionary therapeutic approach that views the psyche as composed of multiple "parts," each with its own perspective and protective function. IFS teaches us to approach our inner world with curiosity rather than judgment, helping parts unburden and return to their natural, valuable states.

Stacy Matulis

Alchemy Somatics

Body-centered work that recognizes the wisdom held in our physical form. Through somatic practices, we access memories, emotions, and patterns stored beneath conscious awareness, allowing transformation at the cellular level.

Carl Buchheit

Transformational NLP (tNLP)

An evolution of traditional NLP that works with the unconscious mind to resolve limiting beliefs and patterns at their root. This approach recognizes that lasting change happens when we address the underlying structure of our experience, not just surface symptoms.
