# Site History

Every version of revealandremember.com that has gone live, newest first.

Each entry has a **bookmark name**. To go back to any version, open a new Claude chat in this website folder and say something like:

> "Put the site back to how it looked on November 25th" or "Restore the live-2026-11-25 version."

You'll get a preview first. Nothing goes live until you say so.

You can also roll back yourself in the Vercel dashboard: **Deployments**, pick the version, then **Instant Rollback**.
