Training & Professional Experience

I'm more than the letters after my name — and so are you.

Credentials are not the whole story, and they should never ask you to override your own inner knowing. But I do believe training matters, because healing work asks for real skill, discernment, and care.

I studied psychology at UC Berkeley, worked in neuropsychological assessment at UCSF, and earned my doctorate at the PGSP-Stanford PsyD Consortium, affiliated with Stanford University School of Medicine.

I spent years training in and practicing evidence-based therapy, including ACT, EFT, CPT, CBT, DBT, EMDR, mindfulness-based approaches, and trauma-focused care. I have led mindfulness groups at Stanford, created and led psychoeducational groups for couples, and received specialty training in LGBTQ+ care, trauma/PTSD, and relationship distress. Across individual, couples, and group work, I have supported more than 300 people in their healing and recovery.

I mention this because, while my work has evolved, this is my background. I know the manuals. I just also know from the inside what feels truer to me.

Where My Work Lives Now

These days, my work lives at the intersection of psychology & soul.

I draw most directly from Internal Family Systems, somatic healing, and Transformational NLP, infused with my own intuition and energetics.

What these approaches share is a belief I hold deeply: you are not broken.

Your deeper Self — your soul, essence, and inner knowing — remains whole and intact beneath the trauma, conditioning, and survival strategies you've carried. My work is about helping you reconnect with that wholeness, heal the young parts of you that got stuck in the past, release what is no longer yours, and reclaim your power as the conscious co-creator of your life.
